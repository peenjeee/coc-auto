CoC Auto for Windows

1. Extract the entire ZIP (right-click it, then Extract All).
2. Open LDPlayer and Clash of Clans.
3. Double-click CoC Auto.exe. Your browser opens the dashboard.

Keep the launcher window open while using the app. Close it to quit.
In Device settings, follow the setup checklist, then check your village.
Python, Git, and Tesseract do not need to be installed.

Already have an installation? Close CoC Auto, double-click
"Import existing setup.cmd", and select the OLD installation folder.
The importer backs up existing destination files and keeps the old folder untouched.

To update: close CoC Auto and extract the newer release into a new folder.
Your saved settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CoC Auto.

Help: https://github.com/peenjeee/coc-auto/blob/main/docs/user/troubleshooting.md
